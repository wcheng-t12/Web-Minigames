📖 How to Play
Step 1 of 5
A Customer Walks In
Each round a customer arrives with items to buy. Their receipt lists every item and the total cost. Check the left panel!
Step 2 of 5
Read the Receipt
The receipt shows the total, how much the customer paid, and the change due. Your job: make up that exact change amount!
Step 3 of 5
Pick Coins & Bills
Click any coin or banknote to add it to your tray. The running total updates as you go. Click a placed item to remove it.
Step 4 of 5
Timer & Lives
You have 3 lives and a countdown timer each round. Wrong change or running out of time costs a life. Lose all 3 — game over!
Step 5 of 5
Earn Up to 3 Stars ⭐
Your final score earns 0 to 3 stars. Finish all 10 rounds with high accuracy to claim the full 3-star rating and become a Cashier Legend!
