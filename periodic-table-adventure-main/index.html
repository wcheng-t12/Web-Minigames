How to Play
Drag or swipe anywhere on the track to slide your insertion cursor left and right.
On desktop you can also use the ← → arrow keys, and Space/Enter to DOCK.
Read the number on the incoming element, then slide your cursor to the gap where it belongs in your line.
Compare it to the elements already docked — bigger goes further right, smaller goes further left.
Tap DOCK once you're lined up. Some rounds sort by GROUP number instead of atomic number — same idea either way.
Not sure which is bigger? Watch for the ← → arrows on the tiles next to your cursor, and the gap glows green when you're in the right spot.
Watch your Round, Score, Lives and Timer at the top of the screen.
Your final stars are based on your score, not your remaining lives.
