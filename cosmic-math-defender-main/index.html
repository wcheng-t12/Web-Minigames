Step 1 of 5
Shoot the Correct Answer
A math problem appears at the top of the screen. Meteors fall carrying different numbers — only one holds the correct answer. Move your ship left/right and fire your laser to destroy it before it reaches the bottom!
Step 2 of 5
Controls
Use ← → Arrow Keys or A / D to pilot your ship. Press SPACE or click to fire your laser. Hit P to pause mid-game, or M to mute the music.
Step 3 of 5
Lives & Scoring
You start with 6 lives. Every wrong shot or missed meteor costs a life and −200 points. Hit the correct answer for big points — chain them together to build a combo multiplier for even bigger rewards!
Step 4 of 5
10 Rounds, 3 Waves Each
The game spans 10 rounds of 3 waves each, covering topics from basic arithmetic up to advanced algebra. Meteors get faster, more answer choices appear, and the math gets harder. Clear all 3 waves to finish a round.
Step 5 of 5
Star Rating at Game Over
When your 6 lives run out or all rounds are cleared, the mission ends and you earn 0 to 3 stars based on correct hits, score, and lives remaining. Aim for 3 stars — the highest honour in Cosmic Math Defender!
