Meet Your Particles
Every atom is made of three particles. Protons (red) and neutrons (blue) live in the nucleus. Electrons (yellow) orbit the outside.
Drag & Drop Assembly
Drag protons & neutrons into the glowing nucleus. Drag electrons anywhere onto the orbital shell rings. Match the target element shown at the top!
Score, Timer & Lives
Complete atoms faster for a speed bonus. Wrong placements cost a life. Lose all lives and the game ends early. Earn up to 3 stars based on total score!
Difficulty Increases Each Round
No difficulty selector needed — the game automatically gets harder every few rounds. More decoy particles, shorter timers, and trickier elements await. Good luck, scientist!
