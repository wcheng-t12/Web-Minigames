How To Play

Pull & Release

Touch the crate and drag backward like a slingshot. Release to fire the crate forward. Pull further = more force!

Friction Changes Everything

Ice is slippery — the crate slides far with little push. Sand is rough — it stops quickly, so you need much more force.

Mass Matters

Heavier crates need a much stronger pull to travel the same distance as light ones. Feel the difference!

Land In The Zone

Too weak, the crate falls short. Too strong, it crashes past. Nail the target zone to complete the round!
