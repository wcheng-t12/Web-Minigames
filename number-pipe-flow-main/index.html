Step 1 of 5 — The Goal
Route a number to the target
A starting number enters from the left. Your job: guide it through the pipe network so it equals the target number on the right — by choosing which pipes to use.
Step 2 of 5 — Operations
Every pipe has a math operation
Each pipe applies a math operation to your number as it flows through. Operations grow harder each round:
+3−2×2 ÷2²√
Step 3 of 5 — Selecting
Click a pipe to route through it
Click any available pipe to route your number through it. Node values update live so you can see the result instantly. You can change your mind — picking a new pipe at any junction replaces the route from there onwards.

Step 4 of 5 — Survival
3 lives — retry until correct
You have 3 lives. A wrong answer or running out of time costs one life, but you stay on the same round and retry until you get it right. Lose all 3 and the game ends. Route carefully — speed earns bonus points!
