CELL DEFENDER

A Biology Lab Mini-Game — guide your cell, absorb nutrients, and survive the invading pathogens.

How to Play

  • ◀▶Move your cell left and right using the arrow keys, or drag/touch on the petri dish.
  • NNutrients (green) float down — absorb them to gain +10 points.
  • PPathogens (red/spiky) are harmful — touching one costs you 1 life.
  • ♥You start with 3 lives. Lose them all and the mission ends early.
  • ⏱Survive until the 60-second timer runs out to complete the mission. Difficulty increases over time!
