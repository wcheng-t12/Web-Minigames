HOW TO PLAY
🏭

THE FACTORY

You are an engineer in a Robot Factory. Each robot needs 4 parts: Head, Torso, Arms & Legs. To find the right part number, you must solve an algebra equation.

3x + 2 = 14 → x = 4
🔧

PICK THE RIGHT PART

After solving, click the button showing the correct answer. The part installs on the robot blueprint. Solve all 4 parts to complete the robot!

LIVES & TIMER

You have 3 lives shared across all rounds. Each wrong answer or timeout costs 1 life. Lose all 3 lives and the game ends immediately — no matter which round you're on! Protect your lives!

🤖 🤖 🤖
📈

DIFFICULTY RISES

Each round brings harder equations. Early rounds use one-step equations. Later rounds include two-step equations and negative numbers. Stay sharp!

ROUNDS 1–2: EASY ROUNDS 3–5: MEDIUM ROUNDS 6–8: HARD ROUNDS 9–10: EXPERT
⭐

SCORING & STARS

Earn points for correct answers. Speed bonuses reward fast solvers. At the end, earn up to 3 stars based on your total score!
