🌾 Farm Tycoon

Grow your farm! Crops grow on their own over time — tap a plot when it's ready (✨ glowing) to harvest for coins. Watch out for plots that need water 💧 or have pests 🐛 — respond before time runs out! Spend your coins on upgrades to grow faster, earn more, and automate care.

How to Play

  • 🌱 Each of the 6 plots grows a crop automatically over time (shown by a progress bar).
  • ✨ When a plot's bar is full, it glows — tap it to harvest for coins!
  • 💧🐛 Sometimes a plot shows a water or pest alert with a shrinking ring timer.
  • 👆 Tap the plot before the ring empties to resolve the alert safely.
  • ❌ If the ring empties, you lose a life (❤️) and that plot's growth is set back.
  • 🛒 Spend coins on upgrades:
    • Fertilizer — crops grow faster
    • Sprinkler System — chance to auto-resolve water/pest alerts
    • Quality Seeds — earn more coins per harvest
  • 🚜 Your farm overview grows visually as you buy upgrades!
  • ⏱️ You have 90 seconds. Harvest as much as you can before time runs out!
