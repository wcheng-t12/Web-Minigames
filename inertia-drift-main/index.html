INERTIA DRIFT

A Physics Lab Mini-Game — fire your thrusters, ride your momentum, and learn how Newton's Laws feel from the cockpit.

Flight Briefing

  • ⌨Use arrow keys / WASD to fire thrusters, or press and hold on the viewport to thrust toward your finger/cursor.
  • ↻Your ship has momentum — it keeps drifting after you stop thrusting (Newton's 1st Law), and bounces off the walls.
  • EEnergy cells (cyan hexagons) drift through space — collide with them to gain +10 points.
  • AAsteroids (orange rocks) will knock your ship off course and cost 1 life on impact.
  • ⏱You have 3 lives and 60 seconds. Survive the run to complete the mission — debris gets faster over time!
