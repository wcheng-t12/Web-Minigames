How to Play

Hold the tap to pour liquid up to the dashed target line, then confirm your reading.

Tap containers to add their volume into the mixing vat. Reach the exact target to succeed.

Fill, empty, and pour between jugs to measure out the exact amount using only their fixed sizes.

Your Round, Score, Lives, and Timer stay on screen. Run out of droplets and it's game over.

Your stars at the end are graded on your total score, not on how many lives you had left. Play accurately and fast!
