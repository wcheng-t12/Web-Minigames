Welcome to the Lab!

You're a lab technician who must balance the scales using chemical bottles. Make both sides equal — and the experiment succeeds!

How It Works

The left pan is fixed — it shows a number you must match. Tap bottles from the shelf to add them to the right pan. When your total equals the left side, the scale tips into balance!

Pick Your Bottles

Bottles on the shelf have different values. Tap to add a bottle to the right pan. Your running total shows on the pan. Go over the target — and you must clear and retry!

Bonus Bottle: ×2

From round 4, a ×2 Catalyst bottle appears. Use it before another bottle to double that bottle's value. This helps you hit targets you couldn't reach with normal bottles!

Scoring & Stars

Balance faster for a time bonus. Use fewer bottles for a precision bonus. You have 3 lives — a wrong answer retries the same round. Complete all 10 rounds for up to 3 stars!
